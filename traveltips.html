Bali Travel Tips

Follow these useful travel tips to enjoy a safe and memorable trip to Bali.

Travel Tips

  1. Carry a valid passport and necessary travel documents.
  2. Carry enough drinking water while visiting tourist places.
  3. Wear comfortable clothes and footwear.
  4. Respect Balinese culture and temples.
  5. Keep your belongings safe while travelling.
  6. Carry sunscreen, sunglasses and a hat.
  7. Try local Balinese food and traditional dishes.
  8. Keep Bali clean and avoid littering.

Things to Carry

  • Water Bottle
  • Camera
  • Sunglasses
  • Hat
  • First Aid Kit
